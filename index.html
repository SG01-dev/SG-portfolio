About

Most businesses don't need more AI hype. They need one tool that saves real hours, works for the people using it, and doesn't create new risks. That's the gap I work in.

I'm Gaius Soetan, but everyone calls me SG. I started in IT support at Radio Nigeria, fixing problems for people who just needed their systems to work. That habit stuck: I judge technology by whether the person in front of it can use it.

Today I'm doing an MSc in Managing AI in Business at the University of Salford, and interning on how Salford Business School selects, approves, monitors and retires AI tools. On my own time I'm working through a structured Python-to-chatbot plan, building towards conversational AI for real customers.

I've also worked security, delivery and care roles in the UK. They taught me how to stay calm, read people quickly and keep promises, which turns out to matter a lot when you're asking a business to trust a new system.

How I use AI

AI speeds up my work. It doesn't replace my judgement. This is the loop I run on everything, including this website.

Frame the problem

Before I prompt anything, I write down who it's for, what "done" looks like, and what must not go wrong.

Prompt with context

I give the model the role, the facts, the constraints and examples of good output, instead of a one-line request.

Iterate in small steps

I build in pieces, test each one, and refine the prompt or the code based on what actually happened.

Check it critically

I verify facts, test edge cases, and look for bias, privacy and licensing issues before anything reaches a user.

The assistant on this page runs on Claude, briefed only on my real background, and told to say so when it doesn't know.
